THE CHALLENGE

Intelligence is powerful.
But it's fragmented.

Siloed Ecosystems

Google’s Personal Intelligence stays in Google. Grok lives in xAI. Telco context is trapped in carrier apps. No one sees the full picture.

Vendor Lock-in

Switching AI companions or carriers means losing your history, preferences, and personalized intelligence. True portability is nearly impossible.

Trust & Privacy Risks

Closed systems force all-or-nothing trust. Users have little control over how their most personal context is used across services.

OUR NORTH STAR

One coherent intelligence.
User-controlled. Open by design.

FOUNDATIONAL VALUES

Core Principles

User Sovereignty First

Individuals fully control their intelligence profile, consent rules, and orchestration. Portability and the right to be forgotten are non-negotiable.

Openness & Interoperability

All standards are open, royalty-free, and implementable by anyone. No proprietary walls between intelligence providers.

Privacy by Design

Data minimization, edge-first processing, consent receipts, and cryptographic safeguards. Intelligence should enhance privacy.

Specialization & Competition

Providers compete on excellence within their domain. The forum ensures fair access to the larger ecosystem.

Transparency & Accountability

Open governance, auditable standards, and clear mechanisms for redress. No opaque systems shaping people’s lives.

Multi-Stakeholder Governance

Users, providers, civil society, and regulators all have a voice. Balanced, transparent decision-making.

HOW WE WORK

Multi-Stakeholder Governance

The Open Intelligence Forum operates as a transparent, neutral organization modeled after successful standards bodies (OpenID Foundation, W3C, IETF, GSMA/CAMARA, Linux Foundation).

Steering Committee
Balanced representation from telcos, AI providers, user advocates, academia, and regulators.
Working Groups
Public development of protocols for context exchange, agent orchestration, consent, portability, and certification.
Open Process
All specifications developed in the open with transparent decision-making and broad participation.

Benefits for Everyone

FOR INDIVIDUALS
  • One coherent, proactive intelligence experience across all services
  • Full control, portability, and consent management
  • Best-of-breed providers without lock-in
  • Stronger privacy through competition and standards
FOR PROVIDERS
  • Access to a much larger ecosystem and addressable market
  • Focus on core strengths while participating broadly
  • Reduced regulatory friction via open standards & certification
  • New revenue models around high-quality specialized intelligence
FOR SOCIETY & THE ECOSYSTEM
  • Accelerated innovation through true composability
  • Healthier competition instead of winner-take-all platforms
  • Foundation for the next generation of agentic and multi-agent systems
  • Reduced concentration of power in personal intelligence
THE JOURNEY

Indicative Roadmap

2026
Phase 1: Formation
Legal entity, initial governance, founding members (telcos, AI providers, advocates). Publish vision & principles. Form first working groups.
2026–2027
Phase 2: Standards Development
Draft specifications for Primary Account interfaces, context exchange, agent orchestration, consent & portability. Pilot interoperability projects. Launch certification framework.
2027+
Phase 3: Adoption & Scale
First production implementations. Broad provider adoption. Real user experiences powered by open standards. Expansion into advanced agentic systems and cross-border governance.

This is infrastructure for the next era of human augmentation.
